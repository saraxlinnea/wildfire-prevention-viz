U.S. Wildfire Data · 2015–2026 · Updated May 2026

Less prevention. More fire.
The gap is widening.

In 2025, under the Trump administration, the Forest Service treated 35% fewer acres for wildfire risk than in 2024 — the sharpest single-year decline in recent history. So far in 2026, nearly 1.9 million acres have already burned, running at 194% of the ten-year average before peak fire season begins in summer.
