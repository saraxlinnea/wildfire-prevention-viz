Less prevention. More fire.
The gap is widening.

On the data. Acres burned are total national figures across all land types, sourced from NIFC annual statistics. Forest Service treatment data is available consistently from 2023 onward via NPR's analysis of agency records. The 2026 bar shows acres burned through May 29 and is a partial year. The forecast range of 5.5-8 million acres is AccuWeather's published 2026 seasonal projection. The chart shows two trends in the same period and does not assert direct causation between prevention acres in a given year and fire acres in the same year.
